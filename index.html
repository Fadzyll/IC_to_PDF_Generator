Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. ScanGo PDF runs entirely in your browser. Photos are processed on your own device and are never uploaded or stored on a server.

Is ScanGo PDF free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no ads, no sign-up and no watermark unless you add one yourself.

Can I combine many photos into one PDF?

Yes. Use Picture to PDF to pick many photos at once or snap pages one after another, reorder them, and save everything as a single PDF.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, in any modern browser: Safari, Chrome, Samsung Internet, and on computers too.

Imbas ke PDF terus dari telefon

Buat salinan IC dengan watermark, atau tukar gambar dokumen, resit dan nota kepada satu fail PDF. Percuma, tanpa daftar, dan gambar anda tidak dimuat naik ke mana-mana.

Bagaimana tukar gambar kepada PDF?

Buka Picture to PDF, pilih atau ambil gambar (boleh banyak sekali gus), susun mengikut urutan, kemudian tekan Download PDF. Semua dibuat dalam telefon anda sendiri.
