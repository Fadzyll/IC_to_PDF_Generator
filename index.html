Make a safe, watermarked copy of your IC in seconds

Banks, employers, landlords and telcos often ask for a copy of your MyKad. IC to PDF turns two phone photos into a clean A4 PDF, with a watermark stating exactly what the copy is for.

  1. Snap the front and back of your IC with the camera, or upload photos.
  2. Crop: the card edges are detected automatically and the photo is straightened.
  3. Enhance with scanner-style filters: Auto enhance, Magic colour, Grayscale or B&W.
  4. Watermark it with your purpose, for example “FOR BANK ACCOUNT OPENING ONLY”, and add the date.
  5. Download or share the PDF straight to WhatsApp or email.

Private by design

Your IC photos never leave your phone. There is no upload, no account and no server storing your data. Everything is processed in your browser, and once the page has loaded it even works offline. You can install it on your home screen like an app.

Frequently asked questions

Are my IC photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device. Your photos are never uploaded or stored on a server, and they are cleared when you tap Clear all or close the page.

Why should I watermark a copy of my IC?

A watermark stating the purpose, such as “FOR BANK ACCOUNT OPENING ONLY”, and the date makes it much harder for someone to reuse your IC copy for anything else, like fraudulent loans or registrations.

What size is the IC in the PDF?

Choose Actual size (85.6 × 54 mm, the real card size, best for printing) or Enlarged (130 mm wide, easier to read on a phone). Both are on a standard A4 page.
